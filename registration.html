Registration policies

Payment & confirmation

Registration is complete only after successful payment. The registration system will send a confirmation email.

WSDM 2027 is an in-person conference. Remote participation is not available.

Presenting your paper

Oral papers: an author must present in person. Otherwise, the paper may be reassigned to a poster following advance email consultation and confirmation.

Poster papers: authors are strongly encouraged to present in person. If exceptional circumstances prevent attendance, an author must still complete an eligible registration and arrange for another registered attendee to present the poster.

Member & student rates

Member: you must be a current ACM Professional Member and provide evidence of membership during registration.

Student: you must be enrolled full-time at an accredited educational institution and provide evidence during registration. Student registrations include the Welcome Reception and Banquet but cannot cover papers; students registering to cover a paper must choose an eligible non-student category.

Cancellations & transfers

No refunds are allowed, and registrations cannot be canceled. The only permitted exception is a transfer of the registration to another person in the event of visa denial.

For travel preparation, see visa information and Venue & Hotels.
