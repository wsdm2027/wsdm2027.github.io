SIGIR Student Travel Grant

SIGIR is pleased to offer Student Travel Grants for students presenting accepted Main or Findings papers at WSDM 2027.

USD 1,200stipend towards travel and accommodation
Full registrationfive-day student registration covered

As a special exception, this grant-supported student registration can cover one accepted Main or Findings paper authored by the awardee (but not a second paper).

Eligibility

To be eligible, the student must: (1) be a student author of an accepted Main or Findings paper at WSDM 2027, (2) attend the conference in person and present that paper, and (3) be a SIGIR member (SIGIR student travel grants are only open to SIGIR members).

Please note that these awards are competitive, meaning not every applicant will receive a grant. We will give priority to students presenting accepted contributions on the main conference track, students from underrepresented countries, and students who have not received a grant in the past.

Important Dates

  1. Nov 21, 2026Award application submission deadline
  2. Dec 4, 2026Award acceptance notification
  3. Dec 17, 2026Author registration deadline

All deadlines are 11:59 pm anywhere on earth.

Please do not register yet. If you want to apply, please do not register for the conference just yet as SIGIR Awards cannot reimburse you for the registration. If awarded a grant, you will receive a code for a registration fee waiver.

How to Apply

To be considered for a grant, students must submit an application, and their thesis advisor must complete a statement of support. Both forms must be received by the deadline.
